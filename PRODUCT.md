# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS + Framer Motion + Lucide icons, as specified in the competition brief. Three.js / React Three Fiber only if a genuinely valuable 3D moment is approved. No project scaffold exists yet.

## Users

- **Attendees** — founders, developers, investors, creators and first-time Web3 visitors deciding whether to attend and then registering.
- **Speakers** — practitioners looking for a speaking slot or applying to speak.
- **Sponsors and partners** — companies and communities evaluating reach and applying to partner.
- **General visitors** — press, community members and competition judges exploring history, themes and organisation.

## Product Purpose

Redesign of the official Web3 Carnival website (https://web3carnival.world/) for the Kalakriti "Design Your Heart Out" competition. The site must answer five questions fast: what Web3 Carnival is, what event is happening, why attend, who participates, and what to do next. Primary conversions: Attend, Speak, Partner, Get Involved. A company overview deck in the same identity is a second deliverable.

## Positioning

Web3 Carnival is a festival-format series, not a single conference: a flagship week (Palm Meadows, Bengaluru, 4–10 December 2023) with daily side events, followed by satellite events in Dubai, Singapore, Delhi and across India. The "Carnival" name and multi-event format are what neighbouring "Summit / Week / Conf" events cannot claim.

## Operating Context

- Organised and "Powered by" Threeway Studio (contact@threewaystudio.world).
- Registration and event pages live on lu.ma; calls on Calendly (calendly.com/web3carnival); applications on Tally forms.
- Socials: WhatsApp group, Telegram (t.me/web3carnival2023), X (x.com/web3carnival), Instagram (instagram.com/web3carnival), LinkedIn (linkedin.com/company/web3carnival), Linktree (linktr.ee/web3carnival2023).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Seven themed "Cons": Blockchain & its Infrastructure, DAO & Governance, Metaverse & GameFi, ZK & Security, CeFi DeFi & Staking, Enterprise Blockchain, NFT & Utilities.
- Programmes: Demo Night (startup showcase for investors), Awards (about 15 past categories, self and peer nomination), "Empowering Women, Accelerating Startups" pillar.
- Application paths: Sponsor, Speaker, Media, Community Partner, Volunteer, Super Demo, Affiliate.
- **Undecided / unconfirmed:** next edition date, city, venue, agenda, ticket pricing. Must render as clearly labelled placeholders.

## Brand Commitments

- Official logo: navy (#020B25) square tile with stacked white "WEB3 / CARNIVAL" wordmark. Keep unchanged; any new treatment is labelled as a proposed secondary lockup.
- Current typeface Mona Sans is a continuity thread.
- The existing Tailwind purple (#7E22CE) is dropped by decision of the team (2026-09-26).
- Chosen creative direction (2026-09-26): MIDWAY festival-poster identity with the INTERSECTION role-filter / agenda-matrix discovery layer.
- Palette (2026-09-27, fifth iteration, team asked for soft colours): "Soft Carnival" — lilac #DCD3F5 hero field, plum ink #1F1B2E for text and the few dark sections, warm white #FAF8F4 page, pastel section tints (butter #F7E9B0, mint #CDE9DB, blush #F6D5CF, sky #CFE0F5), violet #5B3FA8 for links. Earlier red/cream, riso blue/pink, marigold/ink and ink/tangerine were rejected. The logo colour is not a palette constraint (team decision).

## Evidence on Hand

- About 70 named past speakers with role, organisation, location and headshot (hosted on the current site).
- Event archive of 14 listed events (2023–2025) with dates, venues and lu.ma links.
- Site-stated figures, not independently verified: 5000+ attendees, 250+ investors and accelerators, 1000+ Web3 developers, 500+ KOLs, 750+ partners, 1500+ potential Web3 startups. Present as "as reported by Web3 Carnival".
- Sponsor, VC, community and media partner logos on the current site.
- **Absent:** real event photography, confirmed next-edition details, sponsor logo source files. Do not fabricate; use labelled placeholders.

## Product Principles

1. Answer "what, when, where, next step" in the first viewport, always.
2. Welcoming before impressive: a first-time visitor must never feel Web3 jargon is the price of entry.
3. Every event is real and dated, or visibly marked as a placeholder.
4. One path per intent: Attend, Speak, Partner, Get Involved each have a single, obvious route.
5. The carnival is many events, not one; the design shows the whole programme, not only the main stage.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: contrast, keyboard navigation, visible focus, alt text for every speaker and event image, reduced-motion support, 44px touch targets on mobile.
